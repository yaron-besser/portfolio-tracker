## 13D's core macro thesis

**We are entering a stagflationary regime — the biggest stagflation shock in
50 years.** Inflation is accelerating while economic momentum decelerates.
The 40-year financialization era is ending; the era of physical-supply
constraint is beginning.

Triggering event: **US/Israel attack on Iran (2026-02-28)** + closure of
Strait of Hormuz. But this is the catalyst, not the cause — the cause is 40+
years of underinvestment in physical infrastructure, supply chains, and
strategic stockpiles.

## Their 10 conviction points (Q1-2026)

1. **Stagflation regime confirmed.** OECD projects CPI 4.2% US, 4.0% G20 for 2026.
2. **Sovereign debt crisis risk rising** — rising yields meet record sovereign issuance + aging demographics. US 5y5y real yields at 15-year highs.
3. **Fed will be forced to ease** despite inflation. Powell quoted on the "Hobson's choice." Bullish gold, bearish USD.
4. **60/40 falls out of favor.** Bloomberg 60:40 Stock:Bond Index -3% Q1.
5. **🚨 Mag 7 / US tech at major reversal risk** while resource sectors offer "generational buying opportunity." Top-10 AI companies = 40%+ of S&P, matching Nifty Fifty 70s, Japan 80s, dot-com peak weights.
6. **Energy stocks positioned to outperform** after extreme low vs S&P 500.
7. **Critical minerals is the most poorly understood theme** they've ever covered.
8. **US has lost trust of geopolitical allies** — bearish USD.
9. **"War is inflationary"** — defense spending + critical metals scramble. US defense stockpiles inadequate.
10. **China benefits from Middle East conflict.**

## Bookstaber / "I Predicted 2008" thesis (cornerstone quote)

Richard Bookstaber (NYT, 2026-03-16): *"This time, the danger isn't financial
engineering. It's that our financial system has attached itself to the
vulnerabilities of our physical world — power grids, water, land, supply
chains — and created hazards that markets have no framework to analyze...
By the time warning signs show up in market data, the damage will already
have been done."*

13D's framing: 2008 = financialization risk → solvable by central banks
printing money. 2026 = physical-supply risk → NOT solvable by money printing.

## What could be wrong (the bear case 13D isn't making)

- **They've been bearish on tech / bullish commodities for many years.** Same
  thesis 2020, 2021, 2022. Got crushed in 2020-21. Looking right now, but
  "broken clock" risk is real.
- **Iran War de-escalates faster than they expect.** "Greatest oil crisis in
  history" framing collapses if Hormuz reopens within weeks.
- **AI productivity dividend is real** and offsets stagflation pressure
  through margin expansion in non-resource sectors. 13D dismisses this but
  doesn't engage with the productivity case seriously.
- **Mag 7 concentration ≠ inevitable crash.** Comparing to Nifty Fifty / 2000
  ignores that AI cap-ex is real revenue for NVDA / AMD / hyperscalers
  TODAY, unlike dot-com vapor.
- **Their gold $10,000 target requires Fed to capitulate on inflation.** If
  the Fed holds the line and accepts recession, gold could fall (deflationary
  shock).
- **23% cash position is itself a bet.** If the Mag 7 keeps grinding higher
  through stagflation (margin pricing power story), 23% T-bills earning 4-5%
  underperforms massively.
- **Self-fulfilling narrative concern:** "Most contrary view on the planet"
  is becoming consensus. Hard assets crowded trade as of Q1-2026 per the
  +23.4% commodities Q1 return.
