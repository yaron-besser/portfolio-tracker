Source: Q1-2026 Review.

---

## 13D's thesis

- **UN's 2026 World Water Development Report:** world has entered an era of
  **"global water bankruptcy"**
- **~75% of global population** lives in water-insecure nations
- **Rivers, aquifers, glaciers depleting faster than regeneration**
- **Half of world's 100 largest cities** in high water-stress zones
  (UN-Habitat); set to worsen as urban populations grow
- **Iran conflict exposes water infrastructure fragility** — March 7-8 missile
  strikes damaged desalination plants on Iran's Qeshm Island and in Bahrain
- **Bahrain depends on desalination for ~95% of potable water** — neighboring
  Gulf states similarly reliant
- **Water infrastructure as strategic vulnerability** during wartime
- **Tech innovation addressing scarcity** — Norwegian Flocean commercializing
  first commercial-scale subsea desalination (-40-50% energy vs land-based)
- "Water Technology Trends 2026" themes: generative AI for predictive
  maintenance, agent-based architectures, real-time early warning networks,
  cybersecurity for water utilities, expanded public-private partnerships

## What could be wrong

- **"Global water bankruptcy" is a thesis that's been around for 30+ years.**
  Tradeable signals are slow-moving
- **Water utility returns are regulated** — slow growth, dividend stories,
  not high-conviction returns
- **Desalination remains energy-intensive.** Even with Flocean's claimed
  40-50% reduction, scaling subsea desal globally is a 10-20 year story
- **Politically sensitive sector.** Water privatization is controversial in
  many countries; capital allocation gets distorted
- **The 13D Water Index Q1 underperformance** (-4.6% vs -4.3% S&P) suggests
  the thesis isn't translating to current equity returns
- **The smallest HCI position (1.05%)** is the cleanest signal that 13D
  itself isn't deploying capital aggressively here — treat as a "watch list"
  theme, not a current buy
- **Index composition opacity.** "Water" can mean Veolia (utility) or
  Xylem (equipment) or A.O. Smith (water heaters) — very different
  exposures bundled together
