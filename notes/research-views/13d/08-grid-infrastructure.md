## 13D's thesis

- **Antiquated grid = critical bottleneck** in developed world
- Drivers: clean-energy integration, energy security, surging electricity
  demand from digital economy
- **US data center pipeline + grid limitations on collision course**
- **Transformer shortage** is now a national-security issue, expected to
  persist until at least 2030 even in optimistic scenarios
- **Grid digitization imminent** — grid-enhancing technologies (advanced
  power-flow control, dynamic line ratings) can increase capacity 20-40%
  on existing transmission
- "Non-discretionary investment theme" — countries must build, can't defer

---

## What could be wrong

- **The thesis depends on AI data-center buildout continuing.** If AI capex
  stalls (training plateau, hyperscaler discipline, models needing less
  compute), grid demand growth slows
- **Regulation = single point of failure.** Permitting, FERC, state PUCs
  are bottlenecks that capex can't solve. Public opposition to transmission
  lines is intense
- **Concentrated buyer base.** Hyperscalers (MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, META) are
  the demand. If they slow capex, the whole picture changes fast
- **Already up +247% since 2021.** Late entry into a long bull
- **Index composition risk.** Names not detailed — could include utilities
  (regulated returns, slow growth) AND equipment makers (cyclical, high
  beta). Different return profiles
- **Behind-the-meter solutions reduce grid dependency.** On-site nuclear,
  gas turbines, solar+storage at the data center bypass the grid. Microsoft
  + Constellation deal is the template
- **Long-tail buildout risk.** Grid projects take 5-15 years. Stocks
  discount expected returns immediately — there's a "show me the EPS"
  inflection that hasn't fully hit yet
