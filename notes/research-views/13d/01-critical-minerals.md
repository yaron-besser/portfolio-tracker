## 13D's thesis

- Critical minerals face **severe structural supply deficits** colliding with
  exponential demand from defense, energy, industrial, and tech sectors
- **China dominates the midstream** (smelting, processing, refining) — this
  is a "weaponization chokepoint" the West is years from fixing
- Higher metals prices = China "exporting inflation" to the West
- **Top of 13D's mineral list:** copper, silver, antimony, tungsten, uranium,
  graphite, indium, gallium, germanium, **heavy rare-earths**
- Defense will become the price-insensitive first customer; other sectors
  get priced out
- Mining CEOs are "the generals of tomorrow"

## Key claims / data points

- **China = 80% of global tungsten supply.** Export-license restrictions
  imposed Feb 2025; exports limited to a select group of companies since
  Dec 2024. Tungsten now on "record-breaking rally."
- **Heavy rare-earths supply almost entirely concentrated in China.** Used
  in high-performance permanent magnets driving drone motors. Scandium
  rare-earth in aluminum-nitride films = data rates / latency for drone
  networks.
- **US announced $12B national commercial stockpiling initiative** + ex-China
  critical minerals trade zone in Feb 2026. 13D says: insufficient until
  reliable ex-China supply is online.
- **Iran war "burn rates" exacerbating shortages** — tungsten, antimony,
  gallium, germanium, rare-earths consumed in munitions
- **30% knocked off global mining industry valuations** in 4 weeks of Iran
  war — 13D frames as buying opportunity (March 26 WILTW)
- **Saudi Arabia long-term bullish** as future refining/processing hub
  (Future Minerals Forum, Riyadh, January 2026). Headwinds: infrastructure,
  talent, water.
- **US mining education funding inadequate.** China: ~50 mining-engineering
  programs, 12,000 students, 3,000 grads/year. US lacks comparable pipeline.

## What could be wrong

- **The thesis is fundamentally about supply chain decoupling.** If China-US
  relations thaw (post-Iran-war deal, new admin policy), Chinese export
  restrictions could be relaxed → near-term prices collapse
- **Critical minerals demand is downstream of defense and EV cycles.** If
  defense spending plateaus or EV adoption slows, demand assumption breaks
- **The "30% pullback = buying opportunity" framing assumes the bull
  thesis is intact.** Could also be the market correctly pricing peak
  geopolitical premium
- **Index composition risk:** equal-weighted, includes early-stage explorers
  with high failure rates. A long-only index will look spectacular in a bull
  run and brutal in a downcycle
- **"Hype is at an all-time high" — 13D's own warning** (Jan 15 WILTW citing
  Cicero's *caveat emptor*). If 13D itself is flagging hype, the easy money
  may already be made
- **Mining permits take 10-15 years.** Even if 13D is right on demand, supply
  response is slow but not infinite. Substitution + recycling + thrifting
  reduce demand over time
- **No retail vehicle is clean exposure.** Most ETFs (REMX, KRBN) are a mix
  of miners, refiners, and processors with different risk profiles
