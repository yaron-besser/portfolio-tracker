## 13D's thesis

**Gold**
- Gold-to-CPI ratio **broke out from 45-year downtrend in Sept 2024** — primary
  technical signal
- **Gold = best indicator of reflation/deflation.** Historical pattern: oil-price
  spikes / financial crises trigger initial gold selloff, then huge rallies
  1-2 years later (1973 OPEC, 1979 Iran, 2000 dot-com, 2008 GFC averaged ~25%
  drawdown then ramp)
- Recent ~$4,100 intraday low fits the 25% drawdown pattern (the "sell on
  the news" after Iran attack)
- **Eventual target: $10,000+/oz** (double or triple from here)
- Fed will be forced to ease → bullish gold
- USD entering long-term downtrend → bullish gold
- **Paper market dying:** physical demand increasingly setting gold/silver
  prices, not COMEX paper

**Gold Miners**
- Mining-stock outperformance vs major US indices has broken out
- AISC margins **exceed $3,000/oz** at spot prices
- XAU FCF per share could grow **5x from 2024 to 2026** (Baker Steel data)
- Gold miners trading at **<0.8x NAV** vs prior-cycle peak of 1.4x — market
  doesn't believe current bullion price is sustainable
- Historical reference: Oct 2008 → Mar 2009, gold +32%, HUI ~2x, S&P -22%

**Silver**
- 5th consecutive year of structural deficit
- Mine supply flat 810-815M oz, 80-90M below 2016 high
- Recycled silver only offset half the mine-supply loss
- **Industrial demand +174M oz since 2016** — twice the mine supply erosion
- Solar + defense applications drive secular demand
- Silver miners outperformed in last commodity bull despite 5x oil rise

## What could be wrong

- **The $10,000 gold target requires Fed capitulation.** If the Fed accepts
  recession to crush inflation (Volcker playbook), gold falls in
  deflationary shock
- **Paper market death is overstated.** COMEX still clears massive volume.
  "Physical market dominance" is a perennial gold-bull thesis that hasn't
  played out in 40 years
- **AISC margins of $3,000/oz are inflation-vulnerable.** Energy costs +
  mining wages eat margins quickly if inflation runs hot
- **Miner outperformance vs bullion is cyclical, not structural.** Miners
  generally underperform bullion across full cycles due to operational
  leverage going the wrong way at the bottom
- **Silver structural deficit has been claimed for years.** Above-ground
  silver stockpiles (LBMA, COMEX, ETFs) are massive — actual scarcity
  manifesting in spot price is a longer process than "deficit" framing
  suggests
- **Solar silver demand is being thrifted aggressively.** Silver use per
  panel has dropped >50% over last decade. Industrial demand growth could
  slow as substitution improves
- **Gold at $4,000+ is already a massive move** from $1,800 ~2 years ago.
  The "generational opportunity" framing is harder to justify after a
  100%+ rally
- **HCI portfolio has 26.6% in this complex** — concentration risk if the
  bull thesis breaks
