⚠️ **Important nuance**: 13D is structurally bearish on Mag 7 / US tech as a
*concentrated equity bet* (see macro.md), but bullish on the

non-US AI (China DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen) over the Mag 7 names themselves.

## 13D's overarching framework

- **Schumpeter's creative destruction** + **Kurzweil's Law of Accelerating
  Returns** = tech progress is exponential, unaffected by wars or recessions
- Sub-themes since 2011 have averaged +483.8% vs +191.2% S&P / +146.4% MSCI
- **GPTs (General-Purpose Technologies) take time** to show measurable
  economic impact — process transformation, reorganization, reskilling needed

## Sub-themes (4 indices)

### 12a. Automation / AI

**Performance:**
- Q1-2026 YTD: -5.4% (vs -4.3% S&P, slight underperformance)
- Since 2015 inception: +900.0% vs +278.2% S&P

**Thesis:**
- Generative AI = new general-purpose technology
- **China advantage**: energy availability + rapid AI adoption + manufacturing
  data moat
- **Memory chip shortage** intensifying (HBM demand from AI)
- **Humanoid robotics moving from R&D to production** — Tesla Optimus Gen 3
  (50-100K units by late 2026), NVIDIA Cosmos 3, Isaac GR00T
- **AI race accelerating**: GPT-5.4 (1M context, computer-use, extreme
  reasoning); Claude Opus 4.6 (sustained agentic coding); DeepSeek V4 in
  development; Alibaba Qwen > Meta Llama in global downloads
- **China 15th Five-Year Plan**: AI mentioned 50+ times, target 90% AI
  adoption across industries by 2030
- **Open-source AI** as Chinese national asset

### 12b. Clean Energy

**Performance:**
- Q1-2026 YTD: **+10.1%** (vs -4.3% S&P, +14.4pp outperformance)
- Since 2017 inception: +759.1% vs +210.8% S&P

**Thesis:**
- Renewables now supply more electricity globally than coal
- Solar + wind = **default new power source** (cost reasons)
- 2024 energy transition investment = **$2.3T**, EV transport ~$900B
- **2025 new wind+solar capacity: 814 GW** (+17% YoY); total installed >4 TW
- Solar = top US capacity addition for 5th consecutive year (43 GW in 2025)
- Strait of Hormuz closure = compelling case for energy security via renewables
- Australia ~50% renewable in 2025; South Australia targeting 100% in 2027
- **Removed Q1-2026:** LONGi (601012), JinkoSolar (688223), Plug Power (PLUG),
  Largo (LGO), NEL ASA, Canadian Solar (CSIQ) — "mounting company-specific
  challenges threatening returns"

## What could be wrong

**Across the umbrella:**
- 13D's "tech progress = exponential, unaffected by anything" framing is
  philosophical not investable. The *stocks* are very much affected by
  business cycles, valuation regimes, capital flows
- **Index composition opacity.** Hard to tell which names drive the +900%
  Automation Index return without component disclosure
- The Q1-2026 underperformance of Automation (-5.4%) + Longevity (-6.1%)
  suggests the sub-themes don't all move together — bundling them risks
  diluting signal

**Specific:**
- **AI**: 13D dismisses Mag 7 but their alternative (Chinese open-source,
  picks-and-shovels) requires the AI thesis to be right *somewhere*. If AI
  capex peaks, all variants suffer
- **Clean Energy**: removed 6 names in Q1 = high churn. Federal rollbacks
  + tariff uncertainty hurt utility-scale solar (-14% in 2025). Energy-
  security framing is good for the thesis but timing remains hard
- **Wireless / 6G**: 6G commercial deployment is 2028-2030. Buying 6G
  exposure now is buying anticipation, not earnings
- **Longevity**: matched S&P over 8 years = no edge. Biotech IPO cycle +
  rate environment matter more than the longevity thesis
