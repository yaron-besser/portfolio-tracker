it becomes a *must invest* year."**
Source: Q1-2026 Review.

---

## 13D's thesis

- **Chinese stocks in early stages of secular bull market**
- H-shares (Hong Kong) bull spreading to A-shares (mainland)
- "Silent bull market in value stocks" → full-blown bull market
- **Contrarian framing:** Western investors asked "is China uninvestable" for
  3-4 years — 13D treats that as music to their ears
- **Anti-deflation supercharger = Iran War.** War is inflationary; China has
  been struggling in deflation for nearly half a decade. Moderate inflation
  = good for China
- **CSI Energy Index broke out of 18-year downward trendline** (Dec 2025)
- **BCOM Index broke out of 17.5-year downward trendline** (Jan 2026)
- **MOF eliminated VAT export rebates for solar products (effective 2026-04-01)**
  = "second wave of anti-deflation campaign"
- **Two trends 13D expects to continue:**
  1. Upstream sectors outperform downstream sectors
  2. Bear market in long-term Chinese Government Bonds (CGBs) continues
- **Now adding consumer discretionary stocks** to the 13D China indices
  (boosting domestic consumption = top of Beijing's 2026 agenda)

## What could be wrong

- **Geopolitical tail risk.** Taiwan, Hormuz blowback, US-China trade tariffs
  can crater Chinese equities overnight
- **Property bust + consumer balance sheets.** Chinese households are
  dealing with a multi-year housing wealth shock. "Boost consumption"
  policies have been announced repeatedly with limited impact
- **Anti-deflation requires policy follow-through.** VAT rebate elimination
  is one move. Genuine reflation requires fiscal stimulus + monetary easing
  + housing recovery — all uncertain
- **Foreign capital flows whiplash.** Western investors flipped from "
  uninvestable" to bullish quickly — could flip back as quickly on a single
  bad headline
- **HK ≠ Mainland.** A-share / H-share divergence has persisted for years.
  Hang Seng underperformance Q1 (-3% to -6%) vs flat 13D China Index
  suggests the H-share retail bull is fragile
- **Reflexivity in commodity-China nexus.** 13D's commodity thesis depends
  on Chinese demand. If China stumbles, commodities stumble — they're not
  independent bets
- **"Must invest year" framing is salesy.** Treat with the same skepticism
  as any "this time is different" pitch
