---

## 13D's thesis

- **Europe building strategic autonomy** by strengthening the single market
  and attracting capital back
- **Trump's foreign policy** (Greenland, tariffs, Iran war without
  consultation) **accelerates** this — ideological rift widening
- **Merz quote (key signal):** "The Middle East war has *nothing to do* with
  NATO and Germany. The United States did not consult us before this war,
  and so we believe this is not a matter for NATO or the German government."
  = sharp break from "coalition of the willing"
- **Capital repatriation thesis** — European capital flowing back to Europe,
  away from US
- **NOT a fire sale** of US assets — rather Europe making itself
  attractive at the margin to incoming capital
- **Three confounders for investors:**
  1. Capital flows from US → Europe + retail equity participation increase
  2. Venture capital boom in Europe (AI startups comparable to US count)
  3. Europe could be the world's largest AI winner via productivity gains
     in legacy industries (manufacturing, autonomous driving, drug discovery)

## What could be wrong

- **"This time Europe gets its act together"** has been predicted for 25
  years. EU integration is slow, fragmented, prone to crises (Brexit,
  sovereign debt 2011, energy 2022)
- **Capital repatriation requires Europe to be attractive.** Slow growth,
  high regulation, demographic decline are still the European story.
  Marginal capital flows might happen — wholesale repatriation is unlikely
- **Trump policy could reverse.** Admin change in 2028 could restore
  Atlanticism, reversing the entire thesis
- **Iran War de-escalation** removes the energy-shock pressure that's been
  forcing European action
- **AI productivity in manufacturing** is a beautiful story but Europe lags
  US + China badly on AI infrastructure, talent, capital. SAP, ASML are
  exceptions, not the rule
- **Index inception 2025-08** — very young. +5.8% in 8 months is fine but
  not enough data to evaluate the thesis
- **Currency dynamics matter.** EUR strength would dampen European equity
  returns for USD-based investors. Hedged vs unhedged exposure decisions
  matter here
