# Cluster: Commodities (demo note)

> **Demo note.** Synthetic text, written to exercise this project's
> note-shape rules against a generated book. Not investment advice.

## Why this cluster exists

A group over the hard-asset themes. They are held for one reason — real
assets behaving differently from financial ones — but they sit in different
sectors and some of them are in no sector at all.

Grouping them is the only way the total is visible. Merging them into one
theme would hide that metals, food and water fail for different reasons.

___

## What would end it

The reason for the group ending rather than any one theme: real assets
stopping behaving differently from financial ones for long enough that the
diversification was imagined.

___

## Why it spans sectors

A theme is allowed to cross sectors, and this one crosses four. The sector
labels are never merged to make a theme tidy — `cluster_id` is why something
was bought, and the sector is what it is. Two questions, two answers.
