# Cluster: Agriculture (demo note)

> **Demo note.** Synthetic text, written to exercise this project's
> note-shape rules against a generated book. Not investment advice.

## Why this cluster exists

Food production, held both downstream through the producers and upstream
through the machinery. It is a hard asset that is not a metal, which is the
point of it next to the metals theme.

___

## What would end it

The producers turning out to track the broad market rather than their own
input costs, which would mean the theme was never a distinct exposure.

___

## Why it is two sectors

The machinery is Industrials and the producers are Materials and Consumer
Staples. That is correct and is not tidied up. A theme that spans sectors is
the normal case here, not an error to be fixed.
