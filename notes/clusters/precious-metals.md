# Cluster: Precious Metals (demo note)

> **Demo note.** Synthetic text, written to exercise this project's
> note-shape rules against a generated book. Not investment advice.

## Why this cluster exists

Metal exposure taken through the miners, because the miners carry more
leverage to the metal price than the metal does.

Held at two depths: the senior producers and, much smaller, the junior end.

___

## What would end it

The metal rising and the miners not following for long enough that the
leverage argument is simply wrong.

Or the operating risk arriving instead — costs, permits, politics — which
ends the position without ever disproving the thesis.
