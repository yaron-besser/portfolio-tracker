# Cluster: Critical Minerals (demo note)

> **Demo note.** Synthetic text, written to exercise this project's
> note-shape rules against a generated book. Not investment advice.

## Why this cluster exists

Industrial metals, as distinct from precious ones. Copper rather than gold:
a demand story about electrification rather than a store-of-value one.

Written down and not yet owned. The theme exists in the record before it
exists in the book, which is deliberate.

___

## What would end it

The supply argument stopping showing up in the price for long enough that
the shortage was always priced in.

___

## A theme with no money in it

An empty theme is a legitimate state, not a gap. It says a decision was
made and not acted on, which `claims.follow_through()` can then measure —
and measuring that gap is more useful than quietly deleting the row.
