# Cluster: Core US (demo note)

> **Demo note.** Synthetic text, written to exercise this project's
> note-shape rules against a generated book. Not investment advice.

## Why this cluster exists

The base the rest of the book is a deviation from. Two broad index funds, two
screened ones, a small-cap line, and one small short against a weak part of
the consumer.

It is the largest theme, and it is the one with the least thinking in it.
That is the correct order of those two facts.

___

## What would end it

Nothing ends an index base. It would stop making sense only if the rest of
the book grew large enough that there was nothing left for it to be the base
of.

___

## What is really in here

Every theme in this book is also owned inside these funds, at index weight,
whether or not that was intended. `exposure.by_sector()` is the only honest
answer to how much — the intent and the exposure are different questions and
this project refuses to make one number answer both.
