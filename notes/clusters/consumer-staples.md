## Why this cluster exists

Breadth at the other defensive end. Demand that does not depend on anyone
feeling confident.

___

## What would end it

The staples premium disappearing — the companies keeping their stable demand
but no longer being paid for it, which is a slower and more likely ending
than demand actually falling.
