## Why this cluster exists

The layer above the hardware. It captures value later and, if it captures it
at all, for longer — a software business does not have to rebuild its
product every cycle.

___

## What would end it

The rerating reversing. Much of what this theme owns is priced on a multiple
rather than on earnings, and a multiple is a decision other people make.

Or the layer turning out not to capture the value at all, with the money
staying with whoever sold the capacity.
