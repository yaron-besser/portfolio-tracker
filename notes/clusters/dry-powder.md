# Cluster: Dry Powder (demo note)

> **Demo note.** Synthetic text, written to exercise this project's
> note-shape rules against a generated book. Not investment advice.

## Why this cluster exists

Cash, and short-dated government paper that behaves like it. A position
rather than the absence of one.

It is the one theme excluded from the equity base, because counting it there
would make every equity weight look smaller than it is.

___

## What would end it

Nothing. Cash does not have a thesis to break.

What it has instead is a cost — whatever was not bought — and that cost is
invisible, which is the only risk worth writing down here.

___

## Patience or avoidance

A large cash line can be either, and the record cannot tell them apart on
its own. That is what `why_not` is for: `still-waiting` is a different state
from `forgot`, and without the column they look identical.
