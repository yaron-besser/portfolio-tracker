## Why this cluster exists

An open theme with nothing in it. The reasoning was written down and no
position followed.

That is a different state from a retired theme, and the record keeps them

___

## What would end it

Nothing has started, so nothing can end. What would stop the theme being
worth holding open is deciding against it — at which point it would be
retired rather than quietly removed.

___

## Why this one still reports a gap

It is active and empty, so the reports ask what is in it. That is correct:
an open theme with no money in it is a question the record should keep
putting, not a row to tidy away.
