# Cluster: Metals (demo note)

> **Demo note.** Synthetic text, written to exercise this project's
> note-shape rules against a generated book. Not investment advice.

## Why this cluster exists

A theme that was held and has been closed. It covered broad metals exposure
before that work was split into the precious and the industrial cases, which
are different bets with different reasons.

The row is retired rather than deleted. Deleting it would delete the only
evidence that the decision was ever made.

___

## What would end it

Already ended. The theme was retired on purpose, so nothing is missing from
it and nothing further can break.

___

## Why a retired theme still has a note

A retired theme is not an incomplete one. Without the distinction it would
sit in the writing queue for ever, asking for a thesis for something that
was closed deliberately — which is exactly the noise this status exists to
stop.
