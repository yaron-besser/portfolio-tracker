- US 10Y AND 30Y yields making progress higher

- **Synchronized = structural, not idiosyncratic** — this is the technical tell
- Daily TNX chart shows a 5-wave Elliott advance from 5/7 → 5/15 close at **4.572%**

### Near-term yield expectations
- TNX hits stall near 4.60% — **doubtful 4.60% gets immediately exceeded**
- Minor 23-38% pullback of the prior range over the next week

### The 30Y is the more important chart
- TYX weekly chart shows **ascending triangle** with resistance near **5.15%** (the 2023 intra-day peaks)
- 5.00% is "purely psychological"; **5.15% is the actionable level**
- **Weekly close above 5.15% = larger spike in long yields underway** → "definite temporary negative" for equities
- Short-term: pullback to 5.00% = support, then larger push higher

### How this transmits to equities
- Friday 5/15: long yields up + equities reversed bearishly on >3:1 negative breadth = the transmission mechanism Newton has been watching for
- If TYX breaks 5.15% weekly before the late-May low forms, the equity pullback could be deeper / extend longer
