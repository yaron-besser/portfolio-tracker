## The REIT upgrade rationale

### What changed
Newton upgraded the entire REIT sector to OVERWEIGHT after Wednesday 5/6 close at RSPR $36.33 (+1.66% on the day). Prior to this date, REITs were not on his OW list.

### Why now
- Six weeks of base-building from early-2026 lows near $32
- Improved momentum
- Long-term rates at resistance and expected to stall — provides rate relief for rate-sensitive REITs
- Fits the "broadening trade" alongside IWM and Copper
- Multi-year resistance at $37 (2024 high) approaching — close to a major technical breakout

### Setup characteristics
- Clean base-building pattern from early-2026 lows near $32
- Momentum has improved meaningfully over the last six weeks (5/6 reference)
- Setup happens just as TYX (30Y) hits multi-year resistance on long end → near-term yield stall provides rate relief

### RSPR vs XLRE — which to use

| Vehicle | Weighting | Tracks |
|---|---|---|
| RSPR | Equal-weight | All S&P 500 REITs equally — gives more exposure to smaller REITs |
| XLRE | Cap-weight | Concentrates in mega-cap REITs (PLD, EQIX, AMT, etc.) |

**Newton's pick is RSPR specifically** — he's making the breakout call on the equal-weight version because that's where the technical setup is.

For my cluster build-out, this is a real decision. Equal-weight (RSPR) vs cap-weight (XLRE) gives different exposure profiles:
- RSPR captures the "broadening" theme — small/mid-cap REITs participating, not just mega-caps
- XLRE concentrates in data-center / tower REITs (already overlapping AI infra theme via EQIX/DLR/AMT)

For diversification AWAY from Tech-heavy book, RSPR may be the better choice because cap-weight XLRE has ~10-15% data centers which correlate with the AI/Tech theme.

## Rate context (the critical macro)

Newton said TYX (30Y) had pressed to multi-year resistance and likely stalls near-term — that stall provides the rate relief REITs need to clear $37.

**This is consistent with his 5/15 view (9 days later):**
- 5/6: TYX hits resistance, near-term stall expected
- 5/15: Same near-term view — TNX backing-and-filling 23-38% of range expected first
- 5/15: But longer-term — bigger yield rally over coming months that could disrupt equities

**Practical implication for REIT entry timing:**
- The rate-relief window is THE near-term backing-and-filling phase (now through ~early June)
- That's the window when REITs can break out before yields rip higher
- After yields break out (TYX > 5.15% weekly per 5/15 note), the REIT trade faces headwinds

**Buy REITs during the rate-relief window, NOT after yields break out.** This argues for being PROACTIVE on the REIT cluster build-out rather than waiting for the dip-buy tranches to fire.
