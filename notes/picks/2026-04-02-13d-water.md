## What it says

- Water is a standing 13D theme with its own index. The 13D Water Index has
  returned +390.6% since inception on 24 March 2016, against +280.5% for the
  S&P 500.   p.60
- ⚠️ Year-to-date it has **underperformed** the S&P 500 by 0.3 percentage
  points: -4.6% against -4.3%.   p.60
- The driver is scarcity: shortages caused by climate change, population
  growth and industrialisation, which threaten food security and economic
  growth and heighten geopolitical risk over shared water.   p.60
- The UN's 2026 World Water Development report declares the world has entered
  an era of "global water bankruptcy" — nearly 75% of the global population
  live in water-insecure nations, and rivers, aquifers and glaciers are being
  depleted faster than they regenerate.   p.60
- Chatham House, February: the UN Water Decade and Sustainable Development
  Goal 6 (universal access to safe water and sanitation by 2030) are both off
  track.   p.60-61
- UN-Habitat: half of the world's 100 largest cities already sit in high
  water-stress zones, worsening as urban populations grow.   p.61
- Water infrastructure is becoming a military target. Missile strikes on 7-8
  March damaged desalination plants on Iran's Qeshm Island and in Bahrain,
  which depends on desalination for nearly 95% of its potable water.   p.61
- The World Resources Institute warns that desalination plants, dams and
  treatment facilities are increasingly targeted in wartime — as nations grow
  more dependent on engineered water systems, those systems become strategic
  vulnerabilities.   p.61
- Technology is one answer: Norwegian startup Flocean is commercialising the
  first subsea desalination facility at scale this year, cutting energy use
  40-50% against conventional land-based plants.   p.61
- Spending is being shaped by generative AI for predictive maintenance,
  agent-based architectures, real-time early-warning networks, cybersecurity
  for water utilities, and public-private partnerships.   p.61
