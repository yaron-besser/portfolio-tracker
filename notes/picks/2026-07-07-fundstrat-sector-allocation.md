### What changed and why

Mark made four rating changes this month — two upgrades in cyclicals (Cons Disc, Industrials) and two downgrades (Comm Services, Materials). The tilt is toward **cyclical rotation** — Industrials-led — with Comm Services losing pace.

- **Industrials (Neutral → OW)** — Restores joint OW with Tom. The sector was **+9.5% since June update, +5.8% relative to SPX**; 73.8% of members above 20D and 74.7% above 200D — the cleanest breadth in the report. Combined with Mark's upgrade, Industrials becomes the largest allocation increase this month (+2.7pp).
- **Consumer Discretionary (UW → Neutral)** — Sector +4.2%, +0.4% relative since last update. 57.4% above 20D, 53.2% above 200D. Not conviction OW; UW removed because the technical bear case dissolved.
- **Communication Services (Neutral → UW)** — Sector +1.8%, but -1.9% relative. Only 39.1% of members above 200D — weakest breadth of any sector. Mark's downgrade takes the tactical weight down materially (-2.3pp).
- **Basic Materials (OW → Neutral)** — Materials +5.5%, +1.8% relative; 61.5% / 69.2% above 20D / 200D. Trim, not reject — Tom keeps OW.
