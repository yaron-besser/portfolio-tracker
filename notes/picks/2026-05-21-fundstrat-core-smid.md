some of the highest-conviction setups — particularly in industrial

## How this list moved vs April

**No composition changes.** Top 5 and Bottom 5 identical to April 2026.

Key data changes (April → May):
- **STRL** absolute since inclusion: was just listed at $487.87 → now $752 (+54% in one month)
- **IESC** since inclusion: $582 → $656 (+12.7%)
- Most of the bottom-of-N list extended losses (KTOS, ELF, MDB) — selling into weakness here is broken
- **NBIS** (#18, Y) — went from 1M +20.5% to YTD +74.8% rel R2500. One of the strongest movers
- **BE** (#17, Y) — extreme momentum (1M +34.5%, YTD +142%); rank 1 in momentum despite being mid-tier on the Y list

**Interpretation:** The SMID list is showing more dispersion than large-cap.
The AI-infrastructure theme is winning hard (FIX, STRL, IESC, BE, NBIS),
while the bottom is getting punished (PATH -46.5%, MDB -50.0%, ARRY -30.2%).
Fundstrat's "industrial buildout" thesis is the cleanest expression in this list.
