## Inflation — "weaker" (Lee's meme slide)

- **June Core CPI MoM −0.02%** (deflation) vs +0.26%e. YoY 2.59% (from 2.85%).
- **Only 7 of 108 items** had a positive inflation contribution in June.
- **68% of CPI basket deflating from peak** (50-yr avg 32%).
- **Shelter** MoM decelerating fast (0.11 latest); YoY 3.28%, near 40-yr avg 3.36%.
- Beige Book (July): "price growth unchanged or slower in **every** District" — disinflationary direction, not deflation.

## Fed
- In 2Q, market repriced from **−2.0 cuts → +1.6 hikes** (a +3.6 hawkish swing), now back to ~1.0 (≈1 less hike). "Market will test the Fed."

## Beige Book (July 2026) — 5 takeaways
1. **Growth broadened** — 11 of 12 Districts growing (vs 10).
2. **Labor market thaw** — 5 Districts modest/solid gains (vs 1 in May); NY hiring for growth. *"Most hawkish change in the report."*
3. **Inflation pressures less intense** at the margin.
4. **Economy increasingly capex-driven, NOT consumer-driven** — demand concentrated in **data centers, defense, machinery, automation, power infrastructure**. Supportive for industrials / electrical equipment / power infra / defense / data-center supply chains; **less supportive for broad consumer cyclicals.**
5. **Consumer growth composition deteriorating** — trading down, delaying purchases, using credit; rural/ag stress rising. (Bank CEOs — BofA/JPM/Wells — still call the consumer "resilient.")

## Margin debt / index path
- **FINRA margin debt +54% YoY** — historically near levels that precede S&P consolidation.
- **Historical composite** (year +1 after three consecutive ≥+20% years): gains **back-ended toward year-end**. Possible **round-trip** (target ~7,300, aspirational 7,700), a **bear dip**, then a **Q4 rally toward 8,000**. S&P currently **7,499**.
