## TL;DR

Three concrete near-term **headwinds** to the BTC thesis emerged this week:

1. **Sovereign yields rising globally** (US 10Y ~4.6%, 30Y >5%) → liquidity drain
2. **MSTR repurchased $1.5B principal of 2029 convertibles** → near-term STRC proceeds going to debt retirement, NOT BTC accumulation
3. **BTC stalled at 200-day MA** → historically a bear-rally supply zone

Sean's bottom line: *"reasonable area to layer in some downside protection."* Not a sell, but reduce immediacy of upside thesis.

## 2 — Strategy (MSTR) debt repurchase

**Filing:** MSTR 8-K announced repurchase of **~$1.5B principal of 2029 convertible notes for ~$1.38B cash** (a discount to par).

**Why it matters for BTC:**
- Strongly implies recent **STRC preferred-stock issuance proceeds went to debt retirement, NOT BTC accumulation**
- Reduces near-term BTC demand from one of the largest structural buyers
- Does NOT invalidate the medium/long-term thesis — Strategy still buying — just slows the immediacy

**Why MSTR did it:**
- 2029 converts had **$672 conversion price** with **put features beginning mid-2028**
- MSTR currently trading $177 — way below conversion
- Bonds trading below par = opportunity to retire overhang cheaply
- Pre-emptive liability management

## 3 — BTC technicals

- BTC again struggling around its **200-day moving average**
- Sean: "historically, during prior bear market rallies, the 200-day moving average has acted as a zone where incremental supply emerges"
- Not a mechanical sell signal, but a real positioning/risk reference

## 4 — HYPE regulatory risk

- CME and ICE pushing regulators to scrutinize Hyperliquid (manipulation, sanctions concerns)
- BUT Coinbase partnership announced may improve HYPE's regulatory positioning long-term
- Net: regulatory risk meaningful but "appropriately compensated within HYPE's risk/reward"
