### "American Exceptionalism" — the structural bull case
Tom frames the U.S. as the only country with all three structural advantages:

| | Prime-age population growth | AI / compute leadership | Energy independence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **USA** | Positive until 2035 | Leader | Net exporter |
| China | Negative | Leader | Importer |
| South Korea | Negative | Hardware partner | Importer |
| Europe | Negative | — | Importer |
| Latin America | Positive | — | Net exporter |

Per Elon Musk paraphrase: *"compute and energy are the only two things that are truly scarce."* The U.S. is the only country with both. Layered on a prime-age (30-49) demographic tailwind through 2035 — the cohort that historically drives stock market booms.

### Three near-term catalysts that could still trigger a Phase-2 drawdown

1. **Fed test under Warsh.** Markets have tested new Fed chairs in 11 of 13 cases historically. Sworn in 2026-05-22; first FOMC under Warsh ahead. Key tension: oil-driven inflation pressure vs AI's deflationary employment/wage effect. April FOMC minutes show hike is on the table.
2. **Oil supply shock from Iran war.** Could push inflation up and lean Fed hawkish.
3. **🚨 The three largest IPOs in U.S. history — SpaceX, OpenAI, Anthropic.**
   - **SpaceX IPO scheduled 2026-06-12** (tomorrow as of report).
   - Pre-money valuation ~$2T = **2.7% of total U.S. equity market** — largest IPO ever (vs Ford 1.1%, Visa 0.8%, FB 0.7%).
   - Historical pattern across the 10 largest IPOs: S&P 1-month median +3.0%, 3-month +4.5%, **12-month -2.1% to -17.2% in 4 of 10 cases** — lockup-period equity supply expansion creates 12-month indigestion.
   - SpaceX alone = $2T new equity supply when lockup expires.

### Tom's net view
"Long-term constructive (American exceptionalism), but the shorter-term picture warrants caution." YE 7,700 unchanged; round-trip risk remains the central expectation.

### What changed and why

**Mark's three changes all express one call: defensive rotation as overbought leadership consolidates.** Both strategists view this as a *consolidation of overbought momentum names*, not a market top.

- **Health Care upgrade (Neutral → OW)**: Mark's trigger was the equal-weight RSPH/RSP breakout above a 5-month downtrend (in place since January) with a **TD Combo 13 exhaustion signal confirmed on June 4**. **XHS** (Health Care Services) broke out to new all-time highs above its 2021 peak. Strong June-August seasonality is the tailwind. Expects relative strength to last into fall.
- **Information Technology downgrade (OW → Neutral)**: Reverses last month's upgrade. Equal-weighted Tech entered June with weekly RSI ~**87.8** after a 3-month run of +45%; RSPT/RSP weekly flashed **DeMark TD 13 Countdown Sell**. The unwind has begun — **semis fell >10% in a single early-June session**. Mark expects Tech to struggle until mid-term elections; stretched names need weeks of base-building. **Not a bearish call — just consolidation; longer-term uptrend intact**, so Neutral (not UW).
- **Industrials downgrade (OW → Neutral)**: AI-build-out beneficiaries within Industrials are linked to the Tech pullback. But XLI/SPY is at multi-year trendline support with a weekly TD Buy Setup one week from completion — "Industrials should be a sector to favor in the months to come" as falling crude helps transports/airlines. **Best read as Industrials losing leadership pecking order, not a negative call**; Tom maintains OW and Industrials remains one of his top 2026 picks. The downgrade ends the double-OW that had been in place since January.
