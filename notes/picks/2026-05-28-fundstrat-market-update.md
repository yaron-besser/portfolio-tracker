## Headline thesis

> **"2026: Resilience and US exceptionalism."** Round-trip possible mid-year (target zone 7,300), but Q4 rally to **SPX 7,700** unchanged. Sentiment shift since Iran War (Feb 2026): markets went from pricing **2.5 cuts** (Feb 27) → **0.9 hikes** by YE (May 21). That's a 3.4-cut swing in ~3 months.

### Tom Lee's 6 pillars
1. **3-phase market** — back-ended gains toward YE
2. **7 Black Swans, lower P/E** — equal-weight SPX at 16.8x vs 17.4x 6.5Y avg ("0.6x lower despite surviving 7 black swans")
3. **US relative position: Compute and Energy** — *only* US has all three: prime-age pop growth (positive until 2035), AI leadership, energy net exporter
4. **Mid-year challenges (Phase 2 driver):** new Fed Warsh testing + longer war (jet fuel/diesel shock) + 3 largest IPOs in US history (SpaceX, OpenAI, +1)
5. **New Fed (Warsh):** prefers to eliminate forward guidance; sees less structural inflation than markets do; tension with "dovish White House" pushing for cuts
6. **Demographic tailwind:** prime-leverage adults (age 30–48) accelerating 2023–2028 → SPX 15,000 by 2030 (CAGR 15%)

### The Iran War sentiment shift (most important macro chart)
- **2026-02-27**: market priced **−2.5 cuts** (2.5 cuts) in 2026
- **2026-05-21**: market priced **+0.9 hikes** in 2026
- **Net swing**: 3.4 cuts worth of hawkishness in 12 weeks
- **Driver**: Iran War (Feb 2026, 7th Black Swan); AI capex now framed as *inflation driver* not deflation channel (April FOMC minutes)

### "Don't time the market" reinforcement
- Since 2016: SPX +14% avg / ex-10-best-days **−9%** → 2,300bp gap
- "Best days" cluster around tape stress — the cost of being out is asymmetric
