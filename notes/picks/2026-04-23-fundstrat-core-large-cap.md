## What this list is

Fundstrat's curated large-cap universe (44 names) with a multi-factor
ranking system. **Top 5** are the highest-conviction near-term ideas;
**Bottom 5** are the lowest-conviction (effectively "avoid"). The middle
35 names are ranked but unprioritized.

**Ranking inputs** (all visible in the summary table):
- **DQM Rank** — Fundstrat's proprietary fundamental/quality composite (lower = better)
- **Momentum Rating** — relative momentum score (lower = better)
- **Price / 20D MA** — % above/below 20-day moving average
- **20D / 200D MA** — short-term trend vs long-term
- **Price > 20D > 200D** — "Y" means in confirmed uptrend (both above)

## Composite ranking methodology (per Fundstrat)

The framework combines:
1. **Fundamental Rank** = DQM Rank (Data Quality Methodology)
2. **Tactical Rank** = Momentum Rating
3. **Trend Rank** = Price>20D, 20D>200D, Price>200D
4. **Technical Rank** = Newton's view

The "Total" Y/N column reflects whether the stock is currently in trend
(Price > 20D > 200D). This is the binary "is this stock working" signal.
