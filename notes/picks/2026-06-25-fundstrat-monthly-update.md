## Counter-arguments / what to weigh against this

- **Fundstrat's Bottom-5 outperformed Top-5 last month** by 4.5pp (large-cap) and 13pp (SMID). The Top/Bottom framework is not currently working as a signal — read this as research input, not signal.
- **PE expansion from 22x → 20x is "less excited" framing for the same price target.** Earnings doing the work, not multiple expansion. If 2027 EPS slips, the 8,000 target slips with it.
- **Fed task-force overhaul could be a vol catalyst, not just a comm style change.** Warsh removing forward guidance reduces predictability.
- **"Back-ended" composite means the round-trip is still a thing Tom expects.** YE 8,000 doesn't mean linear path from here.
