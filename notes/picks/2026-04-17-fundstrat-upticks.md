## Mark Newton's commentary on the changes

### DELL (NEW, +$196.51)
*Reverse Cup and Handle pattern just yielded an upside breakout — promising.*

- Pattern: nearly two-year reverse Head and Shoulders, built since mid-2024
- Breakout on **above-average volume**
- Targets: **$211 initial, $266 secondary**
- Short-term momentum overbought but monthly RSI nowhere near OB given degree of consolidation
- Tactical: dips offer attractive entries; expect "absolute relative strength" vs broader Tech
- Support: $168, $155

### CRWV (NEW, $118.09)
*Lengthy trend breakout argues for continued acceleration in months ahead.*

- Stock has **doubled in the last month**
- Trend breakout from year-long consolidation (since last summer)
- **Huge volume increase** on breakout — supportive of continuation
- Near-term momentum overbought
- Tactical: **buy dips over next 3-5 weeks at $104, then $99**
- Targets: $139 then $187

### HII (DELETED at $396.17)
*Increasing stalling out in momentum likely results in short-term underperformance.*

- Possible H&S forming (no guarantee)
- Geopolitical climate improvement = defense names could give up gains
- Pullback to monthly lows near $366 possible
- Below $366 = larger period of weakness / technical breakdown
- Long-term structure still attractive — but better opportunities elsewhere

### KO (DELETED at $75.18)
*Near-term weakness possible as Consumer Staples fall out of favor.*

- Staples sector turned back down sharply to new relative lows vs SPX
- Elliott Wave pattern suggests another bout of weakness → correction to $71-$72
- "If markets are working well, I don't wish to overweight many stocks within Consumer Staples"
- Prefer **WMT** as the defensive alternative

### PANW (DELETED at $166.97)
*Bottoming process might take time.*

- Part of Cybersecurity / Software cluster hit hard recently
- Bottoming process possible but momentum still weak
- Volume heavy to downside, pattern could lead to new lows into May
- "I don't mind owning $PANW for the next few years" — but laggard for now
- Will revisit (and **CRWD**) when upward acceleration shows

### FSLR (DELETED at $191.65)
*Existing downtrend hasn't begun to show evidence of giving way just yet.*

- Intermediate-term structure not bad
- Pattern in recent months has trended downward
- No immediate prospects for breaking back out
- Will replace with something more technically appealing

### TT (existing, IMPROVED, $460.27 in commentary section)
*Push back to test all-time highs makes this an attractive risk/reward ahead of a coming breakout.*

- Bullish basing pattern forming
- **Needs push >$475 for breakout conviction**
- Two prior rally attempts at this level (so triple test) — makes it attractive
- Targets: $524, $590
- Support: $441, $403

### CSX (existing, IMPROVED, $42.72 in commentary)
*Breakout to new highs makes this a timely one to consider raising exposure to.*

- Just exceeding former highs after successful breakout 2 months ago + month of consolidation
- Pattern positive for further strength
- Similar setup to what happened with DJ Transportation Average
- Already part of Upticks list, but recent technical improvement = potentially better candidate
