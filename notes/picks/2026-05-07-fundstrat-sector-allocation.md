## Market context (per Tom Lee)

### April recap
- S&P 500 closed month at **all-time highs after V-shaped rally**
- April: SPX +10.4%, Nasdaq Composite +15.3%
- Market bottomed **March 30** consistent with prior major US conflicts ("buy-the-invasion" pattern)
- Institutions caught the V-bottom this time (reversal of 2020-2024 pattern)
- Retail still bearish through entire rally — AAII confirms, ICI shows retail raised cash during selloff

### YE 2026 target: 7,700 (unchanged)
Tom Lee's 3-phase 2026 framework:
1. **Phase 1 — Strong start to 7,300** ✅ (DONE — SPX 7,249 on 5/1)
2. **Phase 2 — Mid-year turbulence** — possible **15-20% drawdown** triggered by:
   - Fed transition test (new Chair Warsh confirmation June-July 2026)
   - Delayed oil/petroleum supply-shock price reaction (jet fuel, diesel)
3. **Phase 3 — YE rally to 7,700**
4. **2027 outlook**: "arguably one of the biggest rallies we see in our lifetime"

### Fundamental bull case (the key argument)
- **AI adds 2pp to US GDP per year for next 5 years**
- Translates to **~6% S&P earnings growth from AI productivity alone**
- "Earnings growth without inflation" = the rare bullish combination
- Underlying earnings backdrop has materially strengthened

### Near-term tactical view (Mark Newton)
- SPX/QQQ at **stall point** — DeMark daily exhaustion signals on SPX
- Breadth deteriorated ~3-to-1 negative on 5/4
- Mid-term election year May seasonality = weakest stretch of calendar
- **Base case: SPX consolidates through May, retraces 38-50% of rally off March 30 low, then resumes advance into August**
- **"Hold-and-add" posture, NOT a sell signal**

## Two scenarios that could change this view (per Tom Lee)

1. **Fed transition test** (Warsh confirmation June-July) — 10 of 13 Fed chairs since 1914 saw 10%+ drawdown in first year. Could trigger 15-20% drawdown.

2. **Oil supply-shock delayed reaction** — JPM analyst note: spare capacity + reserves masking real shortage in jet fuel/diesel. Once exhausted, prices adjust → demand destruction. Could compound with #1.

These are explicitly the **mid-year turbulence** scenarios — not unknown
unknowns, but flagged risks. Cash on sidelines is the dry powder for
buying that dip.
