## What it says

**The frame**
- The theme rests on Schumpeter's creative destruction and, since 2006,
  Kurzweil's Law of Accelerating Returns — progress is exponential and
  "unaffected by wars, economic recessions, or depressions."   p.54

**What is happening now**
- The memory-chip shortage is worsening, driven by explosive growth in
  high-bandwidth memory demand.   p.55
- Humanoid robotics moved from demonstrations to factories: Tesla starts
  Optimus Gen 3 production this summer at Fremont, targeting 50,000-100,000
  units by late 2026 and high volume by mid-2027.   pp.55-56
- At NVIDIA GTC 2026 Jensen Huang said "physical AI" has arrived — Cosmos 3
  world models, updated Isaac GR00T humanoid systems, and a Data Factory
  blueprint for synthetic robot-training data.   p.56
- DeepSeek raised its chatbot context to 1M tokens and is developing V4;
  Alibaba's Qwen has passed Meta's Llama in total global Hugging Face
  downloads.   p.55
- China's 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-2030) references AI 50+ times, sets an
  "AI+ action plan" targeting 90% AI adoption across industries by 2030, and
  promotes open-source AI as a national asset.   p.55
- 13D's China edge argument: energy availability, rapid adoption, and a
  manufacturing data moat Western labs "simply cannot replicate."   pp.54-55

**The negative side**
- "The expectations of a quick return on investments in AI might be
  misplaced, as it takes time for GPTs to demonstrate measurable impact due
  to the need for business process transformation, company reorganization,
  and worker reskilling."   p.55
- "While AI's potential is unprecedented, near-term volatility can't be ruled
  out due to lofty market expectations and uncertainty about quick returns on
  investments."   p.56
- 13D is only "cautiously optimistic" on the Automation Index long term —
  not bullish.   p.56
- The Publisher's Letter: they pruned the HCI portfolio "to eliminate any
  holdings impacted by weakness in AI equities or the cut-off of energy
  supplies" — to prepare for "much higher inflation and a possible
  stagflation."   p.5
- "Recent price action — weak MAG 7, weak SPX and strong gold and gold
  miners — may be a harbinger."   p.17
