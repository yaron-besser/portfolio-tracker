## What pops out

1. **Tech is structurally winning** — 98% beat rate (highest of any sector), +51% Y/Y EPS, +28.9% Y/Y sales, +14.3% relative return. This is the cleanest validation of Fundstrat's Tech OW since the 4/23 deck and 5/7 sector allocation upgrade. AI-productivity argument is showing up in actual reported earnings, not just guidance.

2. **Comm Services strong but bifurcated** — +49.5% Y/Y EPS blended, +53% surprise, but only 68% beat rate (32% miss — worst of any sector). The big AI-adjacent names (GOOGL, META, NFLX) are carrying it; long tail is missing.

3. **Healthcare is the lone earnings recession** — only sector with negative Y/Y blended EPS (-2.9%) AND second-worst relative return (-14.2%). Confirms both strategists' Neutral/UW stance and matches Mark Newton's tactical UW. Sector is failing on both fundamentals and tape.

4. **Energy: fundamentals OK, tape destroyed** — +19.3% surprise, 86% beat rate, but **-15.5% relative return** since 3/31. The crude technical breakdown Mark Newton flagged on 5/1 is doing the price damage, not the earnings. This is exactly the split between Tom (OW, fundamentals) and Mark (UW, technicals).

5. **Utilities worst tape (-18%) despite OK earnings** — rotation out of defensives on full display. Matches the "defensives falling out of favor" thesis in the 5/7 deck (Both UW Staples; Healthcare lagging; Utilities tape rolling over).

6. **Consumer Discretionary: huge EPS beat (+46% surprise), weak sales (+9.4%), weak tape (-2.9%)** — the high-margin AI-leveraged DiscDX names are carrying earnings while consumer-services subgroup breaks down (consistent with Mark's UW call on services side).

7. **Consensus was way too low** — Street EPS for 1Q26 was raised **+12.7%** during reporting. The buy-side was positioned for slowdown that didn't materialize. Combined with retail still bearish and $7.8T sidelined cash, this is the "positioning fuel + fundamental tailwind" combo Fundstrat has been laying out.
