## Bear case

It is concentrated in a way its name hides. A handful of names drive most
of the movement, so it behaves less like an index than it looks.

Held alongside a total-market fund and a technology theme, it is the line
most likely to be the same bet twice. The look-through panel exists
largely to answer that question.
