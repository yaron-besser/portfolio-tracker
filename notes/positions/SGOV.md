Cash that earns something. It is a position, not the absence of one, and
it is counted in the book rather than beside it.

## Bear case

Holding cash costs the return of whatever was not bought instead. That
cost is invisible, which makes it easy to hold too much for too long.

It is also not a view. A large cash line can be patience or it can be
avoidance, and the record cannot tell the difference on its own.

## Why cash is in the denominator

Every percentage on the page says which base it is of. Cash included,
cash excluded, and equity only are three different answers to 'how big
is this position', and the page shows all three rather than picking.
