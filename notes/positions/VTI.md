The widest thing in the book. It owns the whole US market, so it is the
line that does not express a view at all — it is what the rest of the book
is a deviation from.

## Bear case

A total-market fund falls with the total market. There is no part of it
that is defensive, because there is no part of it that was selected.

It also quietly duplicates the themes held directly elsewhere. Owning it
means owning some of every theme in this book a second time, at index
weight, whether or not that was intended.

is the base rather than a trade is that it requires no decisions.
