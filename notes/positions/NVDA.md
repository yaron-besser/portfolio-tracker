# NVDA — NVIDIA Corporation (demo note)

> **Demo note.** Synthetic text, written to exercise this project's
> note-shape rules against a generated book. It is not investment advice and
> not anyone's real view of anything.

Held in the AI Infrastructure theme. The position exists to express one
thing: that demand for accelerator hardware runs ahead of supply for as long
as the build-out lasts.

## Bear case

The build-out is capital spending, and capital spending stops. If the
customers decide they have bought enough, the demand that justifies the
position disappears faster than the reported revenue does.

Concentration is the other half. A small number of buyers account for most
of the volume, so this is not a bet on many customers. It is a bet on a few.

## Catalysts

- [ ] Order growth keeps being reported rather than guided
- [ ] Supply constraints ease without demand easing with them

## How it has to be held

Volatile enough that the real risk is impatience rather than the thesis
being wrong. Worth knowing that about the position before owning it, not
after.
