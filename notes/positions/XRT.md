> **Demo note.** Synthetic text, written to exercise this project's
> note-shape rules against a generated book. It is not investment advice
> and not anyone's real view of anything.

The one position held the wrong way round. It is small, and it exists as a
hedge against a part of the economy the rest of the book is long.

## Bear case

A short has no ceiling. The arithmetic is not symmetrical with a long
position and pretending otherwise is how a small hedge becomes the
largest problem in a book.

It also costs money to hold, every month, whether or not it is working.
`borrow_fees.csv` records what that actually came to.

## Catalysts

- [ ] Held only while the borrow cost stays small relative to the hedge

## Why this is recorded differently

The protective level on a short is a buy, not a sale, and it sits above
the entry rather than below it. Both facts are in the level row, because
a report that assumed otherwise would describe this position backwards.
