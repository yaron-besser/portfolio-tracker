# MOAT — VanEck Morningstar Wide Moat ETF (demo note)

> **Demo note.** Synthetic text, written to exercise this project's
> note-shape rules against a generated book. It is not investment advice
> and not anyone's real view of anything.

A screened core holding rather than a market-cap one. The screen selects
for businesses judged hard to compete with, which is a different exposure
from simply owning the biggest companies.

## Bear case

The screen is somebody's judgement, and it is applied by a committee
whose reasoning is not visible. A screen that stops working does not
announce it.

Screened funds also drift toward the index over time as the screened
names grow. The exposure that justified the purchase can erode without
anything changing.
