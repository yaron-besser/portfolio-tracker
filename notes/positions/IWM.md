Small companies — the part of the market neither of the core funds
reaches in any meaningful size.

## Bear case

Small companies are more sensitive to the cost of money than large ones,
and that sensitivity is the whole position whether or not it was the
intention.

A large share of the index does not earn money at all, so this is partly
a bet on financing conditions rather than on businesses.
