The machinery upstream of the same demand the agriculture fund holds
downstream. Two expressions of one theme, held deliberately.

## Bear case

Equipment is a deferrable purchase. In a bad year the orders simply do
not arrive, and the backlog hides that for two quarters.

It is also a financing business attached to a manufacturing one, which
means it carries credit risk that the share price does not advertise.

## Catalysts

- [ ] Equipment orders hold while crop prices hold
- [ ] The financing book does not deteriorate ahead of the orders
