## Why this cluster exists

The bet this cluster expresses, in plain English. Why does it get its own slot
rather than being folded into something else?

If it holds more than one distinct sub-bet, say so — "BTC is a monetary bet,
ETH is a settlement-layer bet" is two theses in one cluster, and they can fail
independently.

___

## What would end it

What would make you close the whole cluster, not just trim a holding? This is
the cluster-level version of "what would prove me wrong", and it is the field
that lets you exit a theme instead of quietly holding its remains.
